## What Book 8 is about
The Raskghar arc. The Raskghar (werewolf-like, strong at the full moon) raid Liscor and the inn, take Gnolls, Ceria and
Mrsha. Their Great Chieftain is **Calruz** (one-armed Minotaur, alive). The adventurers, the Antinium, Pallass and Liscor
strike back. It ends with the battle at the Raskghar camp. Two side threads: Laken, Tyrion and the Goblins in the north,
and Lyonette's class and Pawn.

## Conflicts and loose ends (flagged; fixed in the batch that touches them)
1. **Calruz.** Our data (and a handoff gotcha) says "Calruz stays missing". Book 8 makes him alive. The note and his record
   change in M28.1. He is the same entity as the Book 1 Calruz (the text says so): no merge needed.
2. **The wrecked inn (Book 7).** Book 8 shows the inn working: new windows, tables, glass; boarded windows after raids.
   Treat as repaired. An early event clears `wandering_inn.windows_broken`. The watchtower is used again (Bird's tower).
3. **Jelaqua's body.** Book 7: broken, talks only. Book 8 (5.24): she is in a Drake body. A repair event, early.
4. **Rags.** Book 7 ended with her turning south. In Book 8 she is near Riverfarm and Lancrel on days 145 – 152, hunted
   by Tyrion. No conflict (Book 7 days were guesses). Her level goes 20 → 24 here.
5. **Laken days.** Day 98 – 105 = days 143 – 150. The Liscor chapters have no days: all of the Liscor day numbers above are guesses.
6. **Zevara and Krshia's shop.** 5.28 says Zevara paid for Krshia's shop. Check Book 7 for the shop's fate in the batch.
7. **Selys.** Free, at the Guild, leasing the breastplate (5.24). Consistent with Book 7 5.18.
8. **Griffon Hunt.** A member lost (Corry), Revi and Typhenous joined. Check the Book 7 roster.
9. **Counts disagree in the text** (Gnolls: about 80, 60, 40, 27, "barely twenty"). Events use no exact counts.
10. **Rain.** Starts day 134 – 135; still on at day 164 (5.44). `izril.rains` and `izril.flood` stay on. The text calls the rains
    "weeks"; the flood has no end in Book 8.
11. **Records to fix:** Lyonette (classes), Erin (36), Bird, Headscratcher, Numbtongue, Shorthilt, Badarrow, Rags, Pawn
    ([Acolyte]), Klbkch ("Revalantor", lost his levels earlier), Ksmvr (former Prognugator), Pisces (Bone Horrors),
    Yvlon (bone-reinforced arms, 5.35).
